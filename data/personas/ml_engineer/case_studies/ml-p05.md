# Plate Reader For Lumen Health

**Client industry:** e-commerce · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** computer_vision

## Problem
The client needed a plate reader because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using containerised services, a single-stage object detector, custom training loops with autograd, MLOps. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 10 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 40k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
