# Drift Monitor For Northwind

**Client industry:** real estate · **Year:** 2025 · **Category:** mlops

## Problem
The client needed a drift monitor because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using experiment tracking and a model registry, custom training loops with autograd, model deployment pipelines and monitoring. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 9 weeks.

## Result
The system improved accuracy from 72% to 96%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
