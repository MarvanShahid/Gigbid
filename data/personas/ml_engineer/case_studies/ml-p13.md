# Plate Reader For Harbor & Co

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** computer_vision

## Problem
The client needed a plate reader because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using PyTorch, Docker, MLOps, image classification and detection models, YOLO. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 8 weeks.

## Result
The system raised conversion by 14%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
