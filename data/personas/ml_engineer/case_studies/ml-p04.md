# Policy Search Bot For Quantum Retail

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2025 · **Category:** llm_apps

## Problem
The client needed a policy search bot because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using fine-tuned a pretrained encoder, FastAPI, text classification and entity extraction. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 5 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 32k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
