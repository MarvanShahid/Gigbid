# Energy-Load Forecaster For Zenith Logistics

**Client industry:** media · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** forecasting

## Problem
The client needed a energy-load forecaster because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using XGBoost, custom training loops with autograd, experiment tracking and a model registry, Docker, time-series forecasting. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 11 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $25k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
