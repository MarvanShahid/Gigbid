# Defect Inspection Line For Zenith Logistics

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2025 · **Category:** computer_vision

## Problem
The client needed a defect inspection line because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using image classification and detection models, YOLO, PyTorch, MLOps. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 1623 ms to 239 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
