# Training Pipeline For Harbor & Co

**Client industry:** real estate · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** mlops

## Problem
The client needed a training pipeline because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using custom training loops with autograd, MLOps, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $27k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
