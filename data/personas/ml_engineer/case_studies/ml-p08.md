# Support-Ticket Copilot For Vantage Ai

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** llm_apps

## Problem
The client needed a support-ticket copilot because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using a BERT-style model, text classification and entity extraction, a managed vector database, RAG, LangGraph. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 10 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 37%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
