# Cash-Flow Predictor For Sagebrush

**Client industry:** edtech · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** forecasting

## Problem
The client needed a cash-flow predictor because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using forecasting weekly demand, containerised services, XGBoost, custom training loops with autograd, experiment tracking and a model registry. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $14k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
