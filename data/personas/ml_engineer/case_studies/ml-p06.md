# Cash-Flow Predictor For Homestead

**Client industry:** edtech · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** forecasting

## Problem
The client needed a cash-flow predictor because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using containerised services, forecasting weekly demand, gradient-boosted trees, experiment tracking and a model registry. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 9 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 37k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
