# Demand Forecaster For Mapleleaf

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** forecasting

## Problem
The client needed a demand forecaster because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using experiment tracking and a model registry, PyTorch, forecasting weekly demand. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 51%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
