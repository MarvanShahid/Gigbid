# Delivery Driver App For Tradewise

**Client industry:** e-commerce · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** mobile

## Problem
The client needed a delivery driver app because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using statically typed JavaScript, React Native, Node.js, social login and token-based sessions, GraphQL. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 12 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $8k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
