# Hr Onboarding App For Quantum Retail

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** saas_web

## Problem
The client needed a HR onboarding app because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using subscription billing and card payments, Tailwind CSS, statically typed JavaScript. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 22k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
