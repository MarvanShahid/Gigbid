# Chat Support Widget For Mapleleaf

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** realtime_apps

## Problem
The client needed a chat support widget because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using a JavaScript backend runtime, a component-based SPA, TypeScript. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 30%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
