# Marketplace Checkout For Quantum Retail

**Client industry:** edtech · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** ecommerce

## Problem
The client needed a marketplace checkout because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using a component-based SPA, PostgreSQL, GraphQL, utility-first styling, subscription billing and card payments. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 7 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $10k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
