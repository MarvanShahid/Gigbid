# Team Wiki For Homestead

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** saas_web

## Problem
The client needed a team wiki because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using React, Next.js, utility-first styling, statically typed JavaScript. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 5 weeks.

## Result
The system improved accuracy from 66% to 91%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
