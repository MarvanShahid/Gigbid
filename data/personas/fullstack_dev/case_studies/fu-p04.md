# Booking Saas For Bluepeak

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** saas_web

## Problem
The client needed a booking SaaS because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using statically typed JavaScript, OAuth, utility-first styling, Stripe. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 7 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 2335 ms to 293 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
