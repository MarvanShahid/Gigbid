# Booking Saas For Willow Learning

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** saas_web

## Problem
The client needed a booking SaaS because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Stripe, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, React, statically typed JavaScript. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 9 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 1759 ms to 394 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
