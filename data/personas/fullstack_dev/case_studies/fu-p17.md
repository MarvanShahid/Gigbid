# B2B Ordering Portal For Northwind

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** ecommerce

## Problem
The client needed a B2B ordering portal because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Node.js, a typed query API, PostgreSQL. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 9 weeks.

## Result
The system improved accuracy from 75% to 90%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
