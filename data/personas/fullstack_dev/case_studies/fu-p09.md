# Marketplace Checkout For Parcelly

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** ecommerce

## Problem
The client needed a marketplace checkout because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using a component-based SPA, an app-router React framework, PostgreSQL. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system raised conversion by 13%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
