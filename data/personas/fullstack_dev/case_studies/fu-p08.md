# Team Wiki For Mapleleaf

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** saas_web

## Problem
The client needed a team wiki because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using an app-router React framework, social login and token-based sessions, statically typed JavaScript, React, PostgreSQL. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 992 ms to 132 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
