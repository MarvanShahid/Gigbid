# Expense Scanner App For Quantum Retail

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** mobile

## Problem
The client needed a expense scanner app because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using React Native, OAuth, GraphQL. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 4 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 21%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
