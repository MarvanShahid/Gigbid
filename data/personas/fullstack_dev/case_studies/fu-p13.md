# Headless Storefront For Kitebird

**Client industry:** real estate · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** ecommerce

## Problem
The client needed a headless storefront because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using GraphQL, Stripe, a JavaScript backend runtime, React. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 10 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $16k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
