# Monthly Board Report For Sagebrush

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** reporting

## Problem
The client needed a monthly board report because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using pandas, Microsoft BI dashboards, regression and hypothesis testing, spreadsheet models with pivot tables. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system improved accuracy from 68% to 94%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
