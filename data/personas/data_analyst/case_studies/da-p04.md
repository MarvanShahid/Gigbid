# Marketing Funnel Report For Mapleleaf

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** dashboards

## Problem
The client needed a marketing funnel report because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using hand-written analytical queries, spreadsheet models with pivot tables, Tableau. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 11 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 25k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
