# Warehouse Migration For Homestead

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** data_pipelines

## Problem
The client needed a warehouse migration because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using SQL, dbt, extract-transform-load pipelines, Google's serverless warehouse, scheduled DAGs for data pipelines. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 48%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
