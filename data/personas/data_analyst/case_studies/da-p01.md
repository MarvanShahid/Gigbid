# Warehouse Migration For Orbitly

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** data_pipelines

## Problem
The client needed a warehouse migration because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using scheduled DAGs for data pipelines, versioned transformation models in the warehouse, hand-written analytical queries, a cloud data warehouse. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 11 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 41%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
