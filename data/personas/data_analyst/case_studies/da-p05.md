# Warehouse Migration For Ironclad

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2026 · **Category:** data_pipelines

## Problem
The client needed a warehouse migration because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Snowflake, dataframe wrangling in Python, Airflow, SQL, ETL. I worked directly with the client's ops lead and delivered in 7 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 9k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
