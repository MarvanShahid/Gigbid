# Marketing Funnel Report For Bluepeak

**Client industry:** real estate · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** dashboards

## Problem
The client needed a marketing funnel report because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using semantic-layer dashboards, SQL, Excel. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 7 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 2308 ms to 128 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
