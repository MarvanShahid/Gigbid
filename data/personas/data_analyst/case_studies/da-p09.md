# Orders Elt Pipeline For Ironclad

**Client industry:** real estate · **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** data_pipelines

## Problem
The client needed a orders ELT pipeline because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using extract-transform-load pipelines, BigQuery, dataframe wrangling in Python, Airflow. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 2309 ms to 120 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
