# Churn Analysis For Homestead

**Client industry:** media · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** reporting

I designed and shipped the solution end to end using spreadsheet models with pivot tables, Power BI, statistics. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 5 weeks.
