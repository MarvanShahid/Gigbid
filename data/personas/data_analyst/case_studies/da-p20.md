# Clinic Operations Dashboard For Ironclad

**Client industry:** e-commerce · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** dashboards

## Problem
The client needed a clinic operations dashboard because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using SQL, Tableau, Microsoft BI dashboards, Looker, Excel. I worked directly with the client's founder and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 34%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
