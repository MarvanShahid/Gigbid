# Sales Kpi Dashboard For Lumen Health

**Client industry:** healthcare · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** dashboards

## Problem
The client needed a sales KPI dashboard because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using hand-written analytical queries, Power BI, Tableau, Excel. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 5 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 2k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
