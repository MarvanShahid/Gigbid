# Cfo Pack For Kitebird

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2024 · **Category:** dashboards

## Problem
The client needed a CFO pack because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using interactive visual analytics workbooks, Excel, Power BI, semantic-layer dashboards. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 5 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $22k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
