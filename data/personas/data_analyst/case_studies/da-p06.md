# Onboarding Experiment For Northwind

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** experimentation

## Problem
The client needed a onboarding experiment because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using hand-written analytical queries, regression and hypothesis testing, A/B testing, pandas. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 3 weeks.

## Result
The system handled 33k requests per day. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
