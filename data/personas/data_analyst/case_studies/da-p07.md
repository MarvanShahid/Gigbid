# Cohort Retention Study For Ironclad

**Client industry:** fintech · **Year:** 2025 · **Category:** reporting

## Problem
The client needed a cohort retention study because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Microsoft BI dashboards, SQL, Excel, statistics. I worked directly with the client's product manager and delivered in 8 weeks.

## Result
The system saved about $6k per month. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
