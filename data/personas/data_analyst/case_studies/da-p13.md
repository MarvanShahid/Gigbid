# Event Tracking Pipeline For Harbor & Co

**Client industry:** logistics · **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** data_pipelines

## Problem
The client needed a event tracking pipeline because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Snowflake, scheduled DAGs for data pipelines, extract-transform-load pipelines. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system cut manual effort by 52%. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
