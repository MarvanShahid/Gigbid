# Cohort Retention Study For Tidewater

**Client industry:** e-commerce · **Year:** 2025 · **Category:** reporting

## Problem
The client needed a cohort retention study because their team was spending too much time on manual work and the existing tooling did not scale.

## What I built
I designed and shipped the solution end to end using Microsoft BI dashboards, statistics, spreadsheet models with pivot tables, hand-written analytical queries, pandas. I worked directly with the client's CTO and delivered in 6 weeks.

## Result
The system reduced latency from 1233 ms to 112 ms. The client extended the contract for a second phase.
